summary: Posted on July 20, 2026 by ArgoCon Japan 2026 Organizers CNCF projects highlighted
  in this post A special half-day ArgoCon Japan (1:30pm – 6:30pm) will be held July
  28, 2026 in Yokohama, Japan as a colocated event with KubeCon + CloudNativeCon.
  This is a chance to hear from and meet both end users and leading Argo Maintainers
  to share how the Argo Project is making organizations more effective with Kubernetes.
  This will include AI Agent Orchestration, GitOps Best Practices, Scaling Argo Workflows,
  a chance to participate in shaping the next versions of Argo CD, and so much more.
  The best part of ArgoCon Japan will be the chance to meet and share wisdom with
  other Argo users and the project’s maintainers, featuring talks from end-user companies
  like American Express, Coralogix and others. Argo CD lead, Michael Crenshaw, Intuit
  will be speaking and sharing a proposal for the next version of Argo CD. Other maintainers
  like myself (Dan Garfield, Octopus Deploy), Christian Hernandez, Cisco, and Nitish
  Kumar, Akuty, will also be sharing project insights and meeting with users looking
  for feedback and sharing best practices. ArgoCon has traditionally been limited
  to North America and Europe where most of the project maintainers live. This is
  the first time we’re bringing ArgoCon to Asia and we’re very excited to meet the
  Argo community in person in this important venue. Over 70% of organizations running
  Kubernetes have adopted, or are in the process of adopting Argo ( CNCF Annual Survey
  ). It runs critical infrastructure in almost every industry and it has a critical
  role in AI, software delivery, data processing and much more. There is a lot to
  be excited about happening in our community for us to celebrate! We’re preparing
  to release Argo CD 3.5 , the biggest release since Argo CD 3.0, with hundreds of
  improvements, especially in making ApplicationSets first-class citizens. We’ve also
  just completed the 2026 Argo CD end-user survey and have lots of insights to share.
