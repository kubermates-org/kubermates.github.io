  long-term stability and predictability are not just preferences—they are necessities.
  Recognizing this need, Red Hat is proud to introduce Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL)
  Extended Life Cycle, Premium, a new, stand-alone offering designed to provide easily
  consumable, significantly longer life cycles for your most vital systems. This premium
  offering builds upon the solid foundation of the RHEL Premium subscription, extending
  maintenance beyond the traditional ten year timeline. It is an indispensable solution
  for highly regulated industries such as financial services, telecommunications,
  government, healthcare, transportation, manufacturing, and energy, where operational
  consistency and strict regulatory alignment are paramount. The RHEL Extended Life
  Cycle, Premium offering is engineered to deliver peace of mind through an extended
  support window: 6 years of extended maintenance for even-numbered versions : To
  further enhance stability, the subscription also offers six years of extended maintenance
  for specific minor releases. This enables a stable, static update stream, providing
  extra years of critical security and urgent bug fixes. Organizations can maintain
  a consistent production environment for an extended period without the pressure
  of an immediate minor release upgrade. 14-year lifecycle for a major RHEL version
  : Going beyond the standard 10-year support and maintenance lifecycle, this subscription
  provides an additional four years of extended maintenance. This is crucial for organizations
  facing constraints such as hardware compatibility or complex application dependencies
  that prevent immediate migration to a newer major release. This extended coverage
  includes essential critical-impact security fixes, selected urgent priority bug
  fixes, and continued troubleshooting for the final minor release of a major RHEL
  version. Packages within RHEL : As long as a package is not at end of life, Red
  Hat may provide security fixes for critical, important, and moderate CVEs with a
  CVSS score of 7 or higher, as defined by Red Hat in a Red Hat Security Advisory
  (RHSA), and urgent and selected high priority bug fixes as defined by Red Hat in
  a Red Hat Bug Advisory (RHBA). The RHEL Extended Life Cycle, Premium subscription
  includes the same comprehensive, world-class service level components that define
  the RHEL Premium subscription, so your operations are always supported: 24x7 technical
  support : Gain access to Red Hat''s global support engineers around the clock for
  Severity 1 and 2 issues, with standard business hours coverage for lower severity
  incidents.'
