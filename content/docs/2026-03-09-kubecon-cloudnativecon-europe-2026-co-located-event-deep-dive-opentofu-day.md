  this year? What will the day look like? Should I do any homework first? Find your
  community! Posted on March 9, 2026 by Program chair, Sebastian Stadil CNCF projects
  highlighted in this post OpenTofu Day debuted at KubeCon Europe 2024 in Paris and
  has been held at every KubeCon since. As the infrastructure-as-code ecosystem reaches
  a clear inflection point with recent licensing shifts, industry consolidation, and
  changes to commercial Terraform offerings, organizations are actively evaluating
  open source, community-governed alternatives. As a CNCF Sandbox project with more
  than 10 million GitHub downloads, OpenTofu offers a vendor-neutral foundation that
  the cloud native community can rely on for the long term. DevOps engineers, platform
  engineers, and infrastructure leaders will benefit most from OpenTofu Day — particularly
  those running Terraform in production who are evaluating or planning a migration
  to OpenTofu in 2026. Platform engineering teams building or evolving internal developer
  platforms will find the sessions highly relevant, as OpenTofu is increasingly becoming
  a core building block in that space. The event is also valuable for infrastructure
  leadership looking to understand the broader strategic implications of licensing
  shifts in the IaC landscape. Maintainers and contributors who want to get more involved
  will find this the best single venue to connect directly with the core team and
  community. This marks the fifth edition of OpenTofu Day and the project’s first
  full year as a CNCF Sandbox project. The conversation has moved beyond early momentum
  and initial adoption toward production maturity and long-term sustainability. Sessions
  this year focus on real-world enterprise adoption, including innersource models
  inside large organizations, OpenTelemetry-based performance debugging for infrastructure
  runs, and scaling OpenTofu across complex environments. The speaker lineup also
  reflects a broader and more diverse community, with more end-user enterprises sharing
  experiences alongside project maintainers. OpenTofu Day is a half-day event featuring
  keynote-style talks, interactive panels, community experience sessions, and lightning
  talks.
