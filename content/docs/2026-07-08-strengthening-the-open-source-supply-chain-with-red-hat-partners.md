  Edge computing Infrastructure Applications Virtualization Share Earlier today, Red
  Hat and IBM unveiled two commercial offerings of Lightwell to deliver automated
  vulnerability remediation at scale. However, true security requires a movement—a
  connected network of industry leaders and experts working in lockstep. Vulnerabilities
  in the open source supply chain affect the entire ecosystem, regardless of where
  they originate. That is why the launch of Lightwell is intrinsically tied to our
  global partner ecosystem. With this release, we are establishing a new standard
  for industry collaboration. Modern innovation is built on open source software.
  But as the complexity of the software supply chain grows, so do the vulnerabilities
  that threaten its integrity. Lightwell is built on the belief that securing open
  source is a mission best accomplished together. By tapping into Red Hat’s robust
  partner ecosystem to fuel and extend the reach of Lightwell, we can improve vulnerability
  intelligence, increase supply chain transparency, accelerate remediation, and establish
  consistent security practices. Through this ecosystem strategy, Red Hat is creating
  a systemic approach to security across the stack. From global systems integrators
  to innovative independent software vendors (ISVs), Red Hat’s partner ecosystem is
  rallying behind Lightwell to provide the backporting and patching consistency that
  enterprises require. Already, technology and cloud providers like Amazon Web Services
  (AWS), AMD, F5, GitLab, Intel, JFrog, Microsoft, NVIDIA, Palo Alto Networks, and
  ServiceNow are working with Red Hat to build remediation directly into the application
  layers and infrastructure where customers operate.
