  Less Effort: Configuring Memory Tiering in VCF 9.1 As someone who started their
  career as a networking professional in the late 1990s, I’ve witnessed a non-stop
  evolution in the industry driven by a singular goal: simplification. The early 2000s
  saw the arrival of ubiquitous bandwidth, fueled by material investments in fiber
  optics from Telcos and global utility providers. This shift was paired with a transition
  away from complex protocols like ATM and a strong move toward the scalability and
  simplicity of Ethernet. The introduction of Multiprotocol Label Switching (MPLS)
  further aided in this evolution. The most profound shift came with the successful
  commercialization of Software-Defined Networking (SDN) by Nicira. I personally engaged
  with this transformation, becoming Nicira’s first customer in the Australia and
  New Zealand (ANZ) region in 2012-2013, recognizing early the power of abstracting
  the network control plane. Today, we are in the age of enabling AI and private cloud
  platforms for enterprises. What remains constant across this entire timeline—from
  the rise of fiber to the shift to Ethernet, the implementation of MPLS, and the
  advent of SDN—is the desire to simplify networking. This pursuit is precisely why
  the modern private cloud, powered by solutions like VMware Cloud Foundation (VCF),
  is the logical next step for every networking professional. Leveling up your skills
  to the modern private cloud is the most critical advantage for thriving in this
  evolving landscape. The digital transformation sweeping the globe is a complete
  reimagining of our industry. Nowhere is this shift more evident than in Asia-Pacific
  and Japan (APJ), where the surge in innovation requires teams to manage infrastructure,
  networking, and security as a unified platform—creating the velocity needed to win
  in a digital economy.'
