  Broadcom - Part 2 The New Frontier: Leading the Cloud-Native Evolution The cloud-native
  revolution is no longer a distant projection—it is at the very heart of modern enterprise
  technology. Across the globe, large-scale enterprises are reaching a crossroads:
  how to scale modern applications while maintaining mission-critical compliance.
  In the Asia-Pacific-Japan (APJ) region specifically, this challenge is intensified
  by a diverse regulatory landscape and soaring data demands. Across APJ, rapid economic
  expansion and rising data privacy demands are reshaping application strategy. Nowhere
  is this more evident than in India, where these forces are accelerating the country’s
  role as a primary engine of regional innovation. To stay ahead, senior leaders and
  architects must fundamentally evolve how they design, build, and scale digital systems
  Kubernetes is the foundation of this evolution to modern applications. While the
  shift to cloud-native is essential, mission-critical enterprises often find that
  a hyperscaler-only approach lacks the necessary control. As a result, adopting a
  Modern Private Cloud has become essential for organizations prioritizing data privacy
  and operational consistency. This has created a high-stakes skills gap. According
  to the CNCF (Cloud Native Computing Foundation 36% of organizations cite a lack
  of training as the main obstacle to scaling these modern apps. While the CNCF cites
  a global skills gap, the pressure in India is unique. With India’s public cloud
  market expected to reach $24.2 billion by 2028, the competition for talent is fierce.'
