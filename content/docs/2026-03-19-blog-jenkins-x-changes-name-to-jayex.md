summary: 'Jenkins X changes name to JayeX! Background Why change? What happens now?
  During the early days of Jenkins X the Jenkins part of the name made sense for two
  reasons: Jenkins was used as the main pipeline engine and the development was done
  within CloudBees, the company that also is the main developer of Jenkins. Nowadays
  the Jenkins X name is more of a burden than boon marketing wise: It attracts people
  interested in Jenkins, who will dissapointed. It detracts people that are looking
  for an alternative to Jenkins, who might actually find what this project offers
  attractive. Technically nothing happens immediately. We will by and by replace the
  Jenkins X name with JayeX within the project without affecting the functionality.
  Any remaining integrations with Jenkins should be seen as deprecated though and
  will eventually be removed. We will renew our marketing efforts using the new name.
