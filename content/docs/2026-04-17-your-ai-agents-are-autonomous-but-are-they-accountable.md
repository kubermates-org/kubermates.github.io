  The AI Governance Gap Is Growing Why accountability, not capability, is the real
  bottleneck for enterprise agentic AI, and what security leaders need to do about
  it before regulators force the issue. Every enterprise is building AI agents. Marketing
  has one summarizing campaign performance. Engineering has one triaging incidents.
  Customer support has one resolving tickets. Finance has one processing invoices.
  And increasingly, those agents are talking to each other: calling tools, accessing
  databases, delegating tasks across complex multi-hop chains. But here’s the question
  nobody wants to hear at 3 a. m. when something goes wrong: who authorized that action,
  what policy permitted it, and what’s the full chain of events? For most enterprises,
  the honest answer is: nobody knows. That’s not a governance problem — it’s an AI
  agent accountability crisis. The data paints a stark picture.'
