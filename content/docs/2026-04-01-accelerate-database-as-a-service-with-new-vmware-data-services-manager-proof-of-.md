  VMware Cloud on AWS Deploying a private cloud Database-as-a-Service (DBaaS) is rarely
  just a technical challenge; it’s also an organizational one. The technology behind
  VMware Data Services Manager (DSM) is proven to deliver massive productivity gains;
  however, many organizations find themselves stuck in pilot mode, wondering how quickly
  they’ll realize the value, and whether they have the talent to manage the DSM. To
  bridge this gap and move customers from the lab to full-scale production, we are
  excited to announce the Proof of Value (PoV) for VMware Data Services Manager ,
  delivered by our delivery partner, AxelCore. The AxelCore PoV is a roadmap built
  on the same methodologies used by Broadcom’s Global Technology Organization (GTO).
  Facing a massive database sprawl across 26 divisions, Broadcom IT used DSM on VCF
  to move from legacy management to a production-scale DBaaS environment in just 6
  weeks. Watch the Video Here The result? Over $10 million in annual savings by repatriating
  workloads from high-cost public cloud providers. The AxelCore PoV is designed to
  help you replicate this success within your own environment. What sets this engagement
  apart is its focus on the business outcome rather than just the technical configuration.
  By identifying the specific “legacy debt” in your environment, AxelCore helps you
  quantify the freedom gained by moving to a modern, open-source-first data strategy.
  “As Broadcom’s exclusive delivery partner for VMware Data Services Manager, we built
  our Proof of Value engagement to do one thing—give IT leaders the hard data they
  need to get the green light. We’re talking savings of up to 90% off legacy database
  costs.'
