summary: 'One stage for cloud native, OpenInfra, and AI Call for Proposals (CFP) now
  open Registration details Get involved Posted on April 9, 2026 by epower The cloud
  native, open infrastructure, and AI communities are coming together this year at
  one of Asia’s flagship open source events. KubeCon + CloudNativeCon + OpenInfra
  Summit Asia + PyTorch Conference China 2026 will take place from September 8–9,
  2026 in Shanghai , bringing multiple open source ecosystems onto a single stage.
  The event brings together communities including the Cloud Native Computing Foundation
  (CNCF), OpenInfra, and the PyTorch Foundation. This year’s event features the addition
  of PyTorch Conference China , co-located with KubeCon + CloudNativeCon and OpenInfra
  Summit Asia. The event provides a space for developers, architects, maintainers,
  researchers, and enterprise leaders to exchange ideas, share production experience,
  and explore open source technologies across cloud native infrastructure and AI systems.
  The Call for Proposals (CFP) is now open , inviting submissions for: Presentations
  Panel discussions Tutorials Lightning talks Proposals are expected to address real-world
  challenges and encourage in-depth technical discussion. Suggested topics for proposals
  include: AI / Machine Learning / Agentic AI / Data Systems AI Infrastructure / Accelerators
  / Performance Engineering Platform Engineering / Cloud Native Architecture Application
  Development / Developer Experience Operations / Observability / Reliability Security
  / Privacy / Confidential Computing Networking / Edge Computing / Distributed Systems
  Cloud Infrastructure / Virtualization / Storage Emerging Technologies / Academic
  Research / Advanced Topics Open Source Community / Onboarding / Collaboration Hardware
  Enablement / Diversity You may submit your Call For Proposals here. CFP Deadline:
  May 3, 2026. Registration is now open, with three pass types being offered: Corporate
  Pass – for company-sponsored attendees, including enterprise and government employees
  Individual Pass – for self-funded participants, non-profits, or independent contributors
  Academic Pass – for full-time students and faculty (valid institutional ID required)
  Passes provide access to keynotes, breakout sessions, and event activities. Submit
  a proposal through the CFP Register early to take advantage of discounted pricing
  With multiple open source communities participating, the event offers an opportunity
  to engage across cloud native infrastructure and AI development in one place. Share.'
