  Architecture Native S3 Compatible Object Storage in VMware vSAN for VCF 9.1 We are
  excited to announce the general availability of VMware Workstation 26H1 and VMware
  Fusion 26H1. These updates continue the evolution of our desktop hypervisors, delivering
  critical architectural transitions for Windows users, enhanced visibility for VM
  management, and expanded support for the latest hardware and operating systems.
  This release builds on the foundational changes introduced in our previous 25H2
  update , where we transitioned to calendar versioning and introduced powerful automation
  tools like dictTool. As we move through 2026, we remain focused on ensuring your
  virtualization environment stays modern, efficient, and aligned with industry standards.
  With the 26H1 release, VMware Workstation Pro for Windows is now a 64-bit application.
  This transition ensures that most binaries, libraries, and installer components
  execute as 64-bit processes. By aligning the hypervisor with modern hardware and
  software standards, we are delivering a more stable and performant foundation for
  the most demanding virtualized workloads. Managing a complex library of virtual
  machines requires quick access to information. Building on the organizational improvements
  seen in recent releases, 26H1 introduces new “quality-of-life” features to help
  you stay organized: Lifecycle Timestamps (VMware Workstation & Fusion): Instantly
  identify VMs by their creation and last powered-on timestamps. This simplifies the
  tracking of long-term projects and the cleanup of temporary test environments. Integrated
  Folder Notes (VMware Workstation): We have brought VM notes directly into the folder
  tabs for VMware Workstation. This provides immediate access to critical metadata
  and documentation without requiring you to navigate through individual settings
  menus.'
