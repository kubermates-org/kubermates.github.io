  intelligence Open hybrid cloud Security Edge computing Infrastructure Applications
  Virtualization Share Since February, over 1,500 organizations have used Red Hat''s
  complimentary Digital Sovereignty Readiness Assessment to establish a sovereignty
  baseline in 15 minutes. Today we''re introducing the Digital Sovereignty Readiness
  Appraisal, a workshop-based maturity evaluation that delivers capability-level analysis,
  industry-weighted scoring, facilitation materials, and an actionable transformation
  roadmap. The assessment framework is open source, and we''ve made it available on
  GitHub. Digital sovereignty has moved from a compliance checkbox to a board-level
  mandate. Geopolitical uncertainty, extraterritorial data access laws, and regulations
  such as the EU''s NIS2, DORA, CRA, and the AI Act oblige organizations to ask hard
  questions—not only where data resides, but who can access it during support escalations,
  software updates, and AI training workflows. Many enterprises have started that
  journey with a baseline assessment. They increasingly know their gaps. What they
  need next is a credible path from diagnosis to action. That is the gap the Digital
  Sovereignty Readiness Appraisal is designed to fill. Red Hat''s approach to sovereignty
  has always been "your cloud, your rules": Control, transparency, and portability
  built on open source and open hybrid cloud. The Readiness Assessment gave organizations
  a fast, objective starting point. The Appraisal helps cross-functional teams turn
  that starting point into a prioritized roadmap they can execute.'
