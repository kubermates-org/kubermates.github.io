summary: 'Who will get the most out of attending this event? What is new and different
  this year? What will the day look like? Should I do any homework first? Find your
  community! Posted on March 19, 2026 by Co-Chairs: Paula Kennedy, Stacey Potter and
  Stéphane Di Cesare Platform Engineering Day launched at KubeCon in Paris in spring
  2024 to create space for deeper discussion on platform engineering principles, practices,
  and real-world experiences. Now in its fifth edition in Amsterdam, the event reflects
  growing community demand as internal platforms become critical for accelerating
  software delivery and establishing consistent guardrails for emerging technologies
  such as AI and autonomous agents. Attendees will gain insight into how organisations
  are building and operating cloud native platforms in production today, along with
  perspectives on where the field is heading. Shared lessons from practitioners will
  help teams avoid common pitfalls and make more informed architectural and organisational
  decisions. The program is designed to serve both newcomers to platform engineering
  and experienced practitioners operating platforms in production. Anyone exploring
  or actively working on internal platforms will find relevant technical and organisational
  insights. We have seen an increase in CFP submissions on the topics of Platform
  Engineering and AI, and we are starting to see more organisations able to speak
  about the practical application of AI and how it can deliver value. This year’s
  program includes more sessions focused on the real-world application of AI within
  platform engineering. The day is heavily focused on platform engineering topics,
  but it also includes talks and panels focused on security—particularly how to build
  security and guardrails directly into platforms. This year we will continue with
  the two-track format, allowing us to feature even more great content. We will start
  the day together with an update from the CNCF Platform Engineering Technical Community
  Group, followed by our keynote speakers. After the morning break, we will split
  into two rooms featuring a range of sessions, including breakouts, panel discussions,
  and lightning talks.'
