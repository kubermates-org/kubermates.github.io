  has historically been a fragmented landscape. Every provider speaks a different
  language. Public clouds have their own format. This creates a massive “Translation
  Tax,” where organizations spend a lot of their cloud financial management time simply
  cleaning and normalizing data rather than analyzing or optimizing it. Cloud providers
  expose cost and usage data in different formats, schemas, and taxonomies, forcing
  FinOps teams to spend significant effort normalizing and reconciling data before
  meaningful analysis can occur. FOCUS was created to address this challenge through
  a common cost and usage specification. Figure 1: VCF uses FinOps At Broadcom, we
  believe financial clarity should be a strategic advantage, not a manual burden.
  That is why we are aligning VCF 9.1 with the FinOps Open Cost & Usage Specification
  ( FOCUS ) for private cloud using VCF , the global cost and value standard that
  translates disparate cost data into one single standard to compare. Figure 2: FinOps
  Framework by FinOps Foundation Think of FOCUS as the universal currency exchange
  for cloud cost management. Just as exchange rates allow you to compare prices across
  different countries, FOCUS lets you compare costs across all your cloud providers
  in one universal format. Developed by the FinOps Foundation, this open standard
  eliminates weeks of manual translation, allowing teams to see their entire cloud
  spend in a single, “apples-to-apples” view.'
